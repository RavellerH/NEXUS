Pre-Implementation Issues

Design-level issues identified before any code was written. Will become bugs if not addressed in implementation.

HIGH open

PRE-001 — WhatsApp Parser Format Fragility

WhatsApp .txt export format differs between iOS and Android, varies by locale and OS version, and changes without notice. A single parser written against one format will silently fail or produce malformed chunks on others.

Fix required: iOS format uses [DD/MM/YYYY, HH:MM:SS] with square brackets. Android uses DD/MM/YYYY, HH:MM - . Need format auto-detection at parse time. Build a format version registry and test fixtures for each known variant.
CRITICAL open

PRE-002 — Embedding Dimension Lock-in

ChromaDB collections are locked to the embedding dimension they were created with. If the embedding model is changed after ingestion, the entire collection must be deleted and all documents re-ingested. This is not communicated to the user.

Fix required: multilingual-e5-large = 1024 dimensions. nomic-embed-text = 768 — incompatible. System must check at startup that the configured model matches the dimension of existing collections. Setup guide must warn clearly.
CRITICAL open

PRE-003 — Docker Volume Loss = Total Knowledge Base Loss

If the Docker volume containing ChromaDB data is lost (accidental docker-compose down -v, host disk failure, VPS rebuild), all ingested knowledge is permanently gone. No backup or restore mechanism exists.

Fix required: Daily automated snapshot of ChromaDB + FTS5 data directories. Clear restore documentation. Named volumes in docker-compose.yml to prevent accidental deletion. Off-site backup recommended (same-disk backup doesn't protect against disk failure).

Open Questions

Unanswered questions that block specific design or implementation decisions.

Q4

Setup Service — Done-For-You or Fully Self-Serve?

Important Medium priority

Does the purchase include a done-for-you VPS setup, or is it fully self-serve?

Why it matters: The gap between "self-hosted" and "non-technical PM" is real. Even with a perfect Docker Compose bundle, setting up a DigitalOcean droplet and running first-time setup requires terminal comfort.

Options:
A. Fully self-serve — works if setup guide is very good and one-command setup actually works
B. Paid setup add-on — e.g., Rp 500K–1 juta for first deployment; removes biggest adoption barrier
C. Free setup for first N clients — good for testimonials and learning what breaks

Recommendation: Offer paid setup as an optional add-on. Keep self-serve path working in parallel. Early clients: do setup free to learn failure modes.
Blocks: Onboarding flow design, pricing add-on structure
Q5

Buyer: PM Personally or Company Procurement?

Important Medium priority

Is the actual buyer the PM personally (personal bank transfer / credit card), or their company (invoice to accounts department + procurement process)?

Why it matters: In SEA EPC firms, software purchases above a threshold go through procurement. This means payment by bank transfer (invoice), signed agreement or PO may be required, and the decision maker (PM) is not the budget approver (finance).

Options:
A. Individual buyer → Gumroad/LemonSqueezy/Stripe; fast; low friction; limits deal size
B. Company buyer → Invoice-based; slower; higher deal size; requires invoicing and contract templates

Recommendation: Design for company buyer (invoice + bank transfer). Most EPC PMs in Indonesia will not use a personal card for business software. Build a simple invoice template and WhatsApp-friendly sales flow.
Blocks: Payment flow design, invoicing, sales process
